Apple Cabins Sales Agreement

Binding contract for the fabrication and delivery of modular unit structure.

gavel Terms of Sale

This agreement outlines the definitive terms and conditions under which Squamish Cabins ("Seller") agrees to design, fabricate, and deliver one (1) Apple Cabin modular unit to the designated purchaser ("Buyer"). The total purchase price is locked upon execution of this document and receipt of the initial 30% deposit.

Any alterations to the design specifications post-execution will be subject to engineering review and may incur additional fees. The Buyer acknowledges that modular fabrication involves standard tolerances and minor variations in finish are considered acceptable within industry standards.

verified CSA Certification

The Apple Cabin unit shall be constructed in strict adherence to Canadian Standards Association (CSA) A277 standard for modular construction. Upon completion, the unit will bear the official CSA certification label, verifying compliance with National Building Code requirements for the specified climatic zone.

  • check_circle Structural integrity verified for high wind and snow loads.
  • check_circle Electrical and plumbing systems factory-tested and sealed.
  • check_circle Energy efficiency compliance (Step Code 3 minimum).

local_shipping Delivery Logistics

Delivery coordination requires a minimum of 30 days advance notice. The Buyer is solely responsible for ensuring adequate site access for heavy transport vehicles (minimum clearance 14ft width, 14ft height). Ground preparation and foundation systems must be completed and inspected prior to the scheduled delivery date. Squamish Cabins covers transportation costs up to 100km from the fabrication hub; subsequent mileage is billed at the standard rate.
